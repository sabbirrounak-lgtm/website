Questions

Can I really go to university without GCSEs or A-levels?

Often, yes. Many UK universities and their partner colleges accept mature applicants (usually 21+) onto foundation-year, CertHE and HND routes, based on an interview, a written task or your work and life experience. Each university makes its own decision.

Is it free?

Our guidance is free. Tuition is usually paid by a Student Finance England tuition fee loan, and you may also get a maintenance loan for living costs. These are loans: you only repay them once you earn above the repayment threshold, and the amount depends on what you earn.

Who is eligible for student finance?

Usually UK residents who are British or Irish citizens, have settled or pre-settled status, or have indefinite leave to remain, and who meet the residency rules. If you've studied before, that can affect your funding. We'll check this with you on the call.

How many days a week will I study?

Many courses for working adults are timetabled on 2 days a week, with daytime, evening or blended options. It depends on the course and campus.

I don't live in the UK. Can you help?

Yes. International students follow a different route (Student visa). See the Study in the UK guide and get in touch.

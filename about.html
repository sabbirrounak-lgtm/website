About Md Sabbir Khan

International Partnerships & Student Recruitment Professional · London, United Kingdom

I am Md Sabbir Khan (often just Sabbir Khan), an international education professional based in London. For more than 12 years I have worked in international student recruitment, university and college partnerships and market development, across the UK, Canada, Australia, South Asia and MENA.

My work sits where universities, agents and students meet: building partnerships that grow international enrolments in a way that is compliant, sustainable and good for students.

What I do now

Since September 2024 I have been Director – Partnership and Business Development at Vive Education Group UK Ltd in London. I lead partner engagement across South Asia, MENA and the UK, and have delivered 120% of the annual recruitment target.
